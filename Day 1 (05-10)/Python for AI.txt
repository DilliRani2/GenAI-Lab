14. NumPy vs Pandas Requirement
skills = ["Python", "NumPy", "Pandas", "Scikit_Learn"]

skill_counts = {}

for skill in skills:
    skill_counts[skill] = (df[skill] == "Yes").sum()

skill_counts

Convert to DataFrame:

skill_df = pd.DataFrame(
    list(skill_counts.items()),
    columns=["Skill", "Job_Count"]
)

skill_df

Visualize:

plt.figure(figsize=(9, 5))

sns.barplot(
